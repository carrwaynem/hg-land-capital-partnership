Land sets the margin

The price and terms of the land are locked in before the first dollar goes into the build. Good land buyers win at the purchase, not at the finish.

Capital sets the speed

Raising money one project at a time is slow. A partner with steady access to capital turns "when can we start?" into "we can start now."

Each side fills a gap

Operators build and run. Land and capital partners source and fund. Each side does the part the other finds hard.

Questions worth asking in the room

On land

  • What size and type of parcel is the sweet spot?
  • How do you find parcels before they hit the market?
  • What does the process look like from signed contract to final purchase?

On capital

  • What kinds of funding can you reach, and how fast?
  • How do you prefer to structure it with an operator?
  • What do you need to see before you commit?

The ask: one 30 minute conversation. It is an introduction, not a commitment. Each side keeps its own business, and any terms are worked out directly between the parties with their own advisors.

Get Land and Capital in the Room First

A perspective on why this conversation comes before the next project

The idea: You already know how to run a project well. What limits how big and how fast you grow is rarely skill. It is two things: access to the right land and access to the right capital. One conversation with someone who lives on that side of the table can change the order you build in.

Why this conversation matters

1

Land sets the margin

The price and terms of the land are locked in before the first dollar goes into the build. Good land buyers win at the purchase, not at the finish.

2

Capital sets the speed

Raising money one project at a time is slow. A partner with steady access to capital turns "when can we start?" into "we can start now."

3

Each side fills a gap

Operators build and run. Land and capital partners source and fund. Together, each side does the part the other side finds hard.

What you get from sitting down

  • A bigger pipeline. Access to land opportunities you would not see on your own.
  • Faster starts. Knowing where funding can come from before you need it.
  • Better terms. Agreements shaped by people who do this every day tend to protect everyone at the table.
  • Scale without more overhead. You stay focused on operations while others handle sourcing.
  • A longer runway. One relationship can support many projects, not just one.

Questions worth asking in the room

On land

  • What size and type of parcel is the sweet spot?
  • How do you find and secure parcels before they hit the market?
  • What does your process look like from signed contract to final purchase?

On capital

  • What kinds of funding can you reach, and how fast?
  • How do you prefer to structure it with an operator?
  • What do you need to see before you commit?

What this is, and what it is not

  • It is an introduction and a conversation. No one is committing to anything.
  • Each side keeps its own business and its own decisions.
  • Any terms are worked out directly between the parties, with their own advisors.

The ask: one 30 minute conversation

Come with your goals and your timeline. Leave knowing whether there is a fit. If there is, you will know your next step. If not, you lost half an hour.
